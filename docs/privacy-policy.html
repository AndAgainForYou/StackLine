Privacy Policy

App: Stackline
Developer: AndAgainForYou
Last updated: May 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stackline ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile block puzzle game on Android and iOS. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • Stackline is an offline-first puzzle game.
  • Your high score and saved game progress are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not require an account, login, or registration.
  • The app may show ads through Google AdMob after a game ends.
  • The app uses Firebase Core for basic platform initialization.

2. Information stored on your device

The app may store the following data locally using on-device storage (SharedPreferences):

  • High score
  • Saved game session (board state, score, and related game progress)
  • Selected theme and language preferences

This information stays on your device unless you uninstall the app or clear app data. We do not receive this data on our own servers.

3. Information we do not collect directly

We do not ask for or collect directly from you:

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Precise location
  • Payment information

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Stackline may display banner advertisements after a game ends. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Ad interaction data
  • Approximate location derived from IP address

Google uses this information to serve, measure, and improve ads. For more details, see Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data in advertising.

You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (for example, "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android or "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS).

5. Firebase

The app uses Firebase Core for platform services and app initialization. Firebase/Google may process limited technical information such as device identifiers, app instance data, and diagnostic logs required for service operation.

Learn more: Firebase Privacy and Security.

6. Third-party services

The app may interact with the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Firebase — platform services

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

7. Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Data processed by Google services is retained according to Google's policies and your device settings.

8. Children's privacy

Stackline is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help address the issue.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information stored on your device through standard platform security features. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

10. Your choices

  • Delete local app data in your device settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove locally stored information
  • Adjust ad personalization in your device privacy settings

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us via GitHub Issues.
